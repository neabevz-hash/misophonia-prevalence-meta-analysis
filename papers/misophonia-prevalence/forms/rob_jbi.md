# Risk of bias: JBI checklist for prevalence studies, decision rules for misophonia (v1.0, frozen with protocol v1.0 on 2026-10-10)

Source: Munn et al. (2014, https://doi.org/10.15171/ijhpm.2014.71; 2015, https://doi.org/10.1097/XEB.0000000000000054). Each item: **yes / no / unclear / NA**. The AI assistant drafts each rating with a supporting quote and its location; the draft is stored unchanged. R1 verifies every item against the paper and decides (protocol §14). No total score. Items 2, 6 and 9 feed the sensitivity analyses.

The target population is the population the study claims to describe: a country, a city, students of a university, patients of a clinic.

| # | JBI question | Yes | No | Unclear |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Was the sample frame appropriate to address the target population? | The frame covers the target population: population register, address list, all enrolled students, all consecutive patients | The frame covers only part of it: one class for "university students", social-media users for "general population" | Frame not described |
| 2 | Were study participants sampled in an appropriate way? | Probability sampling or census of the frame | Convenience, snowball, volunteer opt-in, online non-probability panel (including quota-"representative" panels) | Method not described |
| 3 | Was the sample size adequate? | n ≥ 385, i.e., ±3 percentage points at an expected 10%; for a clinical subgroup n ≥ 139, i.e., ±5 points at 10% | Below these | — |
| 4 | Were the subjects and the setting described in detail? | Age, sex, setting, country and dates reported | Two or more of these missing | One missing |
| 5 | Was the data analysis conducted with sufficient coverage of the identified sample? | Missing misophonia data ≤ 10% of respondents, or handled | > 10% missing, not handled | Not reported |
| 6 | Were valid methods used for the identification of the condition? | Interview with explicit criteria (L3), or an instrument with an interview- or ROC-anchored cut-off (MisoQuest ≥ 61, S-Five ≥ 87, SMS-Adult ≥ 50.5, DVMSQ algorithm) or MQ MSS ≥ 7, used in a language version with published validation | Ad hoc items, cut-offs without a published basis, or instrument versions without any validation in that language | Version or threshold unclear |
| 7 | Was the condition measured in a standard, reliable way for all participants? | Same instrument, mode and threshold for everyone; internal consistency or inter-rater reliability reported in the sample or the source | Different modes or instruments across participants without a check | Not reported |
| 8 | Was there appropriate statistical analysis? | Numerator and denominator clear; CI given or calculable; weights used if the design requires them | Percentages that cannot be traced to numerator and denominator | — |
| 9 | Was the response rate adequate, and if not, was the low response rate managed appropriately? | Response ≥ 60%, or comparison of responders and non-responders, or weights for non-response | Response < 60% without handling; open online surveys with no denominator of invited people | Not reported |

Additional coding, not part of the JBI score: `topic_disclosed` (codebook) — whether the invitation named misophonia or sounds, which risks self-selection of affected people.
