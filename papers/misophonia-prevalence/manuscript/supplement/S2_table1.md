## Mapping rules


- **G1. Cumulative thresholds.** A case definition is the number of people at or above a threshold. Counts in separate severity bands are summed upward (≥ mild, ≥ moderate, ≥ severe). A band that is not cumulative ("moderate only") is not a case definition; its count only feeds the sums.
- **G2. Order on the same instrument.** A threshold at or above the instrument's L2 cut-off is L2 (e.g., A-MISO-S ≥ 15, MQ Severity ≥ 11, MQ total ≥ 34). At or above the L1 cut-off but below L2 is L1. Below the L1 cut-off, inside the published subclinical range (e.g., A-MISO-S 1–4, MAQ sum ≥ 2), is L0: described, not pooled. Thresholds are compared on the instrument's own metric; if two versions differ in the number of items, on the mean item score.
- **G3. Study-defined thresholds without a published basis** take the level given by G2 if the instrument has published thresholds; otherwise L1. `threshold_source` = study's own.
- **G4. Combined definitions.** "A and B" takes the higher of the two levels (interview and MisoQuest ≥ 61 → L3; A-MISO-S ≥ 10 and MQ Severity ≥ 7 → L2; screening question and MQ Severity ≥ 7 → L2). "A or B" takes the lower (clinical on any of DVMSQ, MQ, S-Five → L2; subclinical or clinical on any → L1; self-reported diagnosis or DVMSQ → L1). Instrument = `multiple`.
- **G5. Distribution-based thresholds (E3).** Percentiles, median splits, mean ± SD of the same sample, latent classes and clusters are recorded and not pooled. This holds for the same sample in every report of it (the SMS-Adolescent ≥ 49 threshold of Rinaldi et al. 2022 is the 90th percentile of the Multisense sample, so it is E3 in any report of that sample; the DMQ Impairment ranges are E3 in the DMQ development sample). A threshold taken from another sample's distribution (Jakubovski et al. 2022: AMISOS-R > 23 from Jager et al.'s patients) is not E3; G3 applies.
- **G6. Case definition not stated or not determinable** from the report: level `NR`, listed, not pooled.
- **G7. A scale completed by an interviewer** (e.g., A-MISO-S read aloud in a clinical interview) keeps the scale's level. L3 needs explicit diagnostic criteria applied in an interview.
- **G8. Derivation samples.** When a cut-off was derived by ROC in the same report (S-Five in Vitoratou et al. 2023, SMS-Adult in Simner et al. 2024, MSL-CY in Rappoldt et al. 2024), the estimate keeps its level and is flagged in the table of studies. It is not E3, because the cut-off is not a percentile of the sample.
- **G9. Not misophonia-specific.** A category that merges misophonia with phonophobia or another sound intolerance (the "phonophobia/misophonia" category of the Structured Tinnitus Interview), or a misophonia score predicted from a non-misophonia instrument (MQ total predicted from the Adolescent/Adult Sensory Profile), falls under E2 at the estimate level: listed, not pooled.
- **G10. Two-stage designs.** When a scale is given only to people who passed a screening question, a case is positive at both stages and the denominator is everyone screened at stage 1; screen-negatives count as non-cases. If part of the screen-positives did not complete the scale, the estimate is `NR` (G6) unless the report gives a count over everyone screened.
- **G11. Recommended threshold within a level** (protocol §13, selection step 2): the first threshold in the `L2_rule` or `L1_rule` column, which is the interview-, ROC- or impairment-anchored cut-off where one exists. Within L1, a scale-based definition is preferred to self-identification, self-reported diagnosis or single items.
- **G12. JBI item 6** (`rob_jbi.md`). Yes, as listed in `rob_jbi.md`: an interview with explicit criteria (L3), MisoQuest ≥ 61, S-Five ≥ 87, SMS-Adult ≥ 50.5, the DVMSQ algorithm, MQ Severity Scale ≥ 7. "Interview- or ROC-anchored cut-off" also includes cut-offs anchored on functional impairment (Möllmann et al. 2023) and on clinical cases (Rappoldt et al. 2024; Rosenthal et al. 2021 for DMQ ≥ 1.8), so yes also covers A-MISO-S ≥ 10, AMISOS-R ≥ 24 (or > 23), MAQ ≥ 24, MQ total ≥ 30, MQ Emotions-and-Behaviors ≥ 19, DMQ Symptom Severity ≥ 1.8 and MSL-CY ≥ 10 / ≥ 9. All of these need a language version with published validation. No: severity-band cut-offs without such anchoring (A-MISO-S ≥ 5, AMISOS-R ≥ 11 or ≥ 21, MAQ ≥ 22 or ≥ 25, MQ Severity ≥ 4, MQ Symptom Scale ≥ 14, SMS-Adolescent ≥ 49), thresholds stricter than an anchored cut-off, screening cut-offs set for sensitivity (DSTS-S ≥ 4), SSfM, and every ad hoc definition. Item 6 is rated on the definition that supplies the sample's primary-outcome estimate (L3, else L2), else its L1 definition.
