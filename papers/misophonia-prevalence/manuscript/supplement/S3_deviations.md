# Supplementary File S3. Changes and clarifications after registration

Protocol v1.0 was frozen on 2026-10-10 (git tag `misophonia-prereg-v1.0`). Every change after that date is listed here with its date, what changed, why, and whether any data had been seen when the decision was made. Clarifications that apply a rule of the protocol without changing it (for example, completing Table 1 for instruments found in the included studies) are listed too, marked as clarifications.

| 2026-10-10 | §2 Registration | The planned PROSPERO registration could not be made. The new PROSPERO form offers only "Intervention review"; "Review of incidence or prevalence" is marked "coming soon". The review is registered on OSF only (https://osf.io/39mq2, DOI 10.17605/OSF.IO/39MQ2). If PROSPERO opens prevalence reviews before data extraction starts, the review is registered there too. | Registry limitation | No. The final search was running; no record had been screened |
| 2026-10-10 | §9–§10 CORE | Clarification. CORE's API matched phrases loosely: in the final search 832 of 2,447 retrieved records had none of the search terms in the title or abstract. As the registered query reads "title or abstract contains the term", `search.py` keeps only CORE records whose title or abstract contains the term (case, accents and plural endings ignored). 1,615 records kept. | Applying the registered query as written | No. Records were filtered before de-duplication and screening |
| 2026-10-10 | §11a De-duplication | Clarification of the component rule. Frontiers supplementary files deposited as `Data_Sheet_1_<article title>.pdf` (also `Table1_`, `Image_2_`, `Presentation_1_`) are treated like "Additional file 1 of …": they are attached to the article with the same title, and the file extension is ignored. | Same kind of record as the listed examples | No. No record had been screened |

| 2026-10-10 | §11a De-duplication | Clarification of rule 3 (pointer sources join the record with the same title). Google Scholar shortens long titles with an ellipsis. This merged 11 of the 23 records that Scholar alone had contributed. | Truncated display titles are the same titles | No. No record had been screened |
| 2026-10-10 | §9 LILACS | LILACS was not searched directly. The BVS search servers refused connections from our network on 2026-10-09 and 2026-10-10, and the BVS API requires a key. The planned retry from a mobile network was not made: the expected yield was a few records beyond SciELO and OpenAlex, which were searched with Portuguese and Spanish terms. This is reported as a limitation. | Access; low expected yield | No. No record had been screened |
| 2026-10-11 | §11b Selection | Clarification of how verification and the safeguard combine. The safeguard was triggered: in the calibration sample the AI labelled exclude 3 records that R1 labelled unsure. R1 first re-screens the 790 AI-excluded records outside the calibration sample blind (6 files, random order, seed 20261011). These records are decided by R1's blind label and are not shown again with AI labels. Verification with AI labels then covers the 567 records outside the sample that the AI sent to full text, and the 33 calibration records where R1's blind label and the AI label differ (both labels shown). Every record thus gets an R1 decision, and no AI-excluded record is decided by agreeing with the AI. | Keeps the re-screening blind; avoids showing records twice | Calibration agreement statistics only; no AI label of a re-screened record |
| 2026-10-11 | §8 I3, §11b–§11c Author contact | No authors are contacted (decision of R1). Full texts come only from open-access sources: Europe PMC, CORE, Unpaywall, OpenAlex, repositories. Reports without an open-access full text are listed as "reports not retrieved" (PRISMA 2020). Eligible studies whose case counts are missing from the published report are listed without pooling, not completed through authors. The possible bias from relying on open-access availability is reported as a limitation. | Single unfunded author; no correspondence | Title/abstract decisions only; no full text read |
| 2026-10-15 | §13 Table 1 | Clarification. Table 1 (`forms/instrument_thresholds.csv`) completed for every instrument in the 119 eligible reports, with the general mapping rules G1–G12 in `forms/table1_completion.md`. The extraction codebook's instrument list gains `DSTS-S`, `MSL-CY`, `SSSQ4`, `SSfM` and `multiple`. | Required by §13 before extraction | Yes: the AI full-text proposals of the 119 eligible reports (instrument names, thresholds, counts) had been read to list the instruments. Levels were set from the instrument papers and the rules, not from prevalence values |
| 2026-10-15 | §11b–§11c Linking, extraction | Clarification of the procedure. (1) Linking: 119 eligible reports form 95 studies (`extraction/study_map.csv`, built by `make_study_map.py` with the reason for every link); 17 groups of 2–5 reports describe the same samples (preprint and article, thesis and article, dataset record and article, secondary analyses of one survey). Pairs that only might overlap (same university or lab, no evidence of shared participants) stay separate and are shown to R1. (2) Extraction unit: one AI job per main report (97 jobs); the other reports of the same samples are attached while the input stays under 400,000 characters per report; each sample is extracted once, from its main report. (3) Drafts are stored unchanged as one JSON line per job in `extraction/draft/<run>.jsonl`. (4) Codes added to `estimates.csv`: level E3, E2 and NR (listed, not pooled) besides L0–L3; field `table1_rule`; instruments `DSTS-S`, `MSL-CY`, `SSSQ4`, `SSfM`, `multiple`. (5) The extraction prompt was piloted on five jobs and frozen as v1.0 (`extraction/extract_prompt.md`, git tag `misophonia-extraction-prompt-v1.0`); the five pilot jobs are re-run with v1.0. (6) R1 verifies in Markdown files (`extraction/verify_md.py`): the drafted values are shown with location and quote, R1 writes corrections and marks each sample verified; the parser applies the corrections to the stored drafts and counts them. | Applies §11b–§11c to the reports found | AI full-text proposals and the five pilot drafts |
| 2026-10-15 | §9 Citation searching, §11b Selection | Change of procedure for the citation search. Backward and forward citation searching of the 119 included reports and four systematic reviews ran through OpenAlex (`search/citation_chase.py`, run `search/runs/2026-10-15-citations/`): 2,081 cited and 1,561 citing works. 119 of the 123 seeds were found in OpenAlex; the reference lists of the other four (three theses and one preprint) were checked by hand against the record set and gave no new study. 1,915 works were not in the main record set, and none had a condition term in the title or abstract: the database search had already found every OpenAlex record with such a term. Instead of title/abstract screening of all 1,915 (§11b), they were searched with the OpenAlex full-text search for the condition terms (misophonia, misophonic, selective sound sensitivity, misokinesia): 674 have a full text in OpenAlex, 140 mention a term. These 140 go straight to full-text assessment (AI proposal with the frozen full-text prompt v1.0, R1 decides every report; records R2001–R2140). The other 1,775 are not screened further. Records without a full text in OpenAlex (1,241) are a limitation. | A study can report misophonia counts without naming misophonia in its title or abstract; screening 1,915 off-topic titles (references on statistics, autism, ADHD, hyperacusis) by hand would add load without finding such studies | Included reports and their metadata; no data of the new records |

| 2026-10-15 | §13, §15c Estimate selection | Added after the first analysis run (post hoc). Selection step 2 (§13) uses the recommended threshold of Table 1, the first in the L2 column. When a study did not report it, step 3 (median logit; with two estimates, the lower) chose the "severe" band over the "moderate or more" band in four samples (AMISOS-R ≥ 31 instead of ≥ 21 in three, MAQ ≥ 43 instead of ≥ 22 in one), because AMISOS-R studies report bands rather than the Youden cut-off ≥ 24. The primary analysis keeps the registered rule. Sensitivity analysis S11 instead takes the next threshold listed in Table 1 before the median rule: 14.1% (95% CI 11.2–17.5) vs 11.9% (9.0–15.6). Also corrected in the code before reporting: the recommended MAQ threshold is ≥ 25, the first in Table 1, not ≥ 24. | L2 is defined as "moderate or higher"; the median rule with two bands picks "severe" | Yes: pooled results of the first run |
| 2026-10-15 | §15b Moderators, §15c S11 | Clarifications and one post hoc addition, made while writing the results. (1) Instrument within L2, listed among the moderators, is tested like the other exploratory moderators: a likelihood-ratio test in the L2 estimates (model with and without instrument, same random part). (2) The share of variance explained by level and instrument (aim 2 of the protocol) is computed as 1 − total variance of the three-level model / total variance of the model without fixed effects (logit scale). (3) Post hoc: S11 is also pooled by population type; the text reports it for the probability samples of the general population (5.6%, 95% CI 3.3–9.1, vs 3.3% with the registered rule), where one of the four changed estimates lies. | (1)–(2) the protocol names these analyses but not the test or the statistic; (3) the lowest probability-sample estimate is a "severe" band chosen by the median rule | Yes: pooled results of the first run |
| 2026-10-15 | §17 GRADE | Clarification. The protocol names the GRADE domains and, for imprecision, the threshold (CI half-width above 5 percentage points). The other thresholds were set when rating, after the pooled results were known: risk of bias, more than half of the inverse-variance weight from samples rated no on JBI item 2, 6 or 9; inconsistency, a 95% prediction interval reaching both below 5% and above 20% (odds ratio: both sides of 1); imprecision for the odds ratio, a CI including 1; indirectness, fewer than five countries or three continents; publication bias, small-study regression p < 0.10, assessed for prevalence outcomes when k ≥ 10 (not for the odds ratio). Certainty starts high for event rates (Iorio et al., 2015). Rules and inputs: Table 4, `manuscript/make_tables.py`, `analysis/results/grade_inputs.csv`. | The protocol gave the domains, not the cut-offs | Yes: all results |
